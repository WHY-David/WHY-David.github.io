This work studies how unitary evolution and measurements jointly shape entanglement and scrambling in random circuits. By mapping the dynamics to a classical spin problem, it analyzes both measurement-induced and measurement-only transitions, derives conditions for globally scrambled phases under joint measurements, and uncovers emergent U(1) and SU(2) symmetries in the large-qudit limit.
